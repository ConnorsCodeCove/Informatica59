## Restoring Connectivity: How We Helped a Construction Company Get Online  

At **Informatica59**, we pride ourselves on being there when businesses need us most. Recently, we received an urgent call from a construction company that was in a tough spot. They had a new building under construction and had hired a company to run the necessary network cabling. Unfortunately, the job was left unfinished, leaving them with an incomplete infrastructure and no clear path forward.  

They needed a reliable IT partner to assess the situation, complete the work, and ensure their network was fully functional. That’s where we came in.  

## Assessing the Challenge  

We quickly scheduled a visit to the site to evaluate the existing cabling and determine what needed to be done to get the network operational. After a thorough assessment, we found multiple issues, including improperly terminated connections and missing key components required for a stable network setup.  

## Scope of Work  

Once we identified the gaps in their network infrastructure, our team took a structured approach to complete the job efficiently:  

- **Cable Termination:** Finished termination of approximately 100 network cables.  
- **Networking Equipment Installation:** Mounted and secured network racks and hardware.  
- **Device Configuration:** Set up and configured critical networking equipment, including:  
  - Wireless Access Points (WAPs)  
  - Switches  
  - Routers, using a **router-on-a-stick** setup for VLAN segmentation.  
- **Conference Room Setup:** Ensured their meeting space had full network functionality, including connectivity for presentations and video conferencing.  

By addressing both physical and configuration-related network needs, we provided the company with a seamless, high-performance setup tailored to their business operations.  

## Taking Over & Completing the Job  

Our team got to work immediately, picking up where the previous company left off. We corrected the wiring issues, installed the necessary components, and ensured all connections were properly terminated. Within the agreed timeframe, we completed the project and got their network up and running seamlessly.  

## More Than Just Cabling – A Long-Term IT Partnership  

But our job didn’t stop there. The company also needed a trusted IT provider to configure their network and manage their systems on an ongoing basis. After reviewing their needs, we provided a tailored managed services plan that fit their budget—at **50% less** than the service provider who initially ran the cables.  

Now, not only does this construction company have a fully operational network, but they also have a dedicated IT partner to ensure their systems remain efficient, secure, and scalable for the future.
